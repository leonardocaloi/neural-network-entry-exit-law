The manuscript is independent and not peer-reviewed. Versions 1–3 are historical
drafts. Their PDFs retain the original layout and wording; source paths were
updated during repository reorganization. The current PDF was rebuilt after
updating artifact references, without changing mathematical conclusions.

The `plot_*.py` scripts generate the accompanying figures. The reproduction guide
distinguishes scripts that simply plot from those that compute a trajectory.
The broader research map is context and proposed work, not an extension of the
theorem's claims or an announcement of completed research in other fields.
