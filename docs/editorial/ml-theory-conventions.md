# Machine-learning theory writing conventions

**Status:** Historical editorial study.

This note surveys manuscript organization, reproducibility statements, limitations, and checklist conventions in machine-learning theory. Its observations were recorded during drafting. Any future submission should use the venue's current instructions rather than treating this historical survey as a binding checklist.
