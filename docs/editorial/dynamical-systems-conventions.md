# Dynamical-systems writing conventions

**Status:** Historical editorial study.

This note compares presentation conventions for short theorem-and-experiment papers in dynamical systems and applied analysis. It covers title and author information, abstract structure, notation, and theorem environments. Publication preparation does not imply submission, acceptance, or compliance with every current venue rule.
