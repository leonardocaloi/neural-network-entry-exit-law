# Trainable readouts: extension scoping

**Status:** Draft extension; not established by the release.

The note investigates the loss of a constant affine live-system matrix when readouts are trained. It explores an averaged-Hessian comparison, residual-curvature bounds, balancing identities, and a candidate modified release coefficient. A later draft and review 18 record additional proof issues. Treat the proposed theorem and extrapolations as unfinished research rather than as conclusions of the main manuscript.
