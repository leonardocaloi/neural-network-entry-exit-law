# A null control invalidates the first MLP campaign

**Status:** Negative result: measurement failure.

The 600-run stage-4a campaign failed its preregistered P-NULL validity rule. Pure diffusion triggered the calibrated test, invalidating the mechanism verdicts. Subsequent diagnosis focused on rapid boundary crossings and heterogeneity between units. This result motivated changes to event detection, stratification, and the deterministic setting. It is evidence about a failed instrument, not evidence against every inactive-unit mechanism.
