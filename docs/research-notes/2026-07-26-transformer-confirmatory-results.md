# Transformer confirmatory study: interpretation

**Status:** Small-network empirical evidence.

The 800-run campaign distinguishes frozen and trainable attention and shallow and deep injected inactivity. The current manuscript reports 22/50 sustained returns in the shallow frozen-attention softplus-beta-3 cell and the hard-ReLU upstream contrast of 20/100 versus 0/100. The historical protocol label about transfer is preserved in the original record; the public interpretation is observational.
