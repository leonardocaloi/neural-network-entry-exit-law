# Stochastic toy v3: mechanism study

**Status:** Historical exploratory interpretation.

The v3 note compares explanations of the stochastic return mechanism and records a residual that scales with temperature. It also records the rejection of a pure-fluctuation reference model at the predictor stage. The historical interpretation is limited to this toy program and does not establish a general stochastic entry-exit theorem.
