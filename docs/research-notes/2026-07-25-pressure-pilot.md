# MLP capacity-pressure pilot

**Status:** Exploratory, rare-event evidence.

The pilot observed dynamical returns under a constrained-width setting and used the results to motivate a dedicated rate study. The small number of events was insufficient for confirmatory rate claims. Its practical contribution was identifying a regime and the instrumentation requirements for the later 4a.3 campaign.
