# Contact and penalty limits: original research question

**Status:** Historical conjectural framing.

The note asks whether sharp-activation training dynamics can be interpreted through penalty limits and unilateral-contact ideas. It sketches candidate conjectures, a route to a proof, and reasons the analogy might fail to provide new mathematical content. This is an origin of the entry-exit project, not a demonstrated equivalence between neural training and a general contact-mechanics system.
