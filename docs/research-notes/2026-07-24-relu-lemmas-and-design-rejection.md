# Verified toy identities and a rejected experiment

**Status:** Historical design failure.

The first ReLU-death cycle obtained useful identities while failing to produce an adequate experimental discriminator. The review separated statements true by construction from predictions that could fail. The next cycle introduced a reduced stochastic model whose proposed quantitative schedule could be tested.
