# Adam pilot: alternative release clocks

**Status:** Exploratory optimizer extension.

The pilot compares candidate release clocks under Adam and records hypothesis-specific outcomes and deviations. It motivates questions about epsilon, accumulated optimizer state, and what a separate confirmatory study would need. These are distinct from the deterministic gradient-flow result in the main paper. The associated protocol artifacts do not extend that theorem automatically.
