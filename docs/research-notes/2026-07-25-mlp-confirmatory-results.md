# MLP confirmatory study: interpretation

**Status:** Small-network empirical evidence.

The 400-run deterministic study reports returns and residual-area observations conditional on dynamical deaths. The current release distinguishes the 15/30 eligible-return estimate from the 13/16 area-opening fraction among all primary-arm returns. The trained network goes beyond the fixed-readout theorem hypotheses. The original note's stronger wording must be read alongside the scope corrections in the publication review.
