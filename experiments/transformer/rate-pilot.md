# Transformer rate pilot

**Status:** Exploratory sizing and instrumentation.

This pilot diagnoses repeated threshold crossings, collapses events by injected unit, and compares attention frozen versus trainable. Return times and depth-conditioned observations informed the 800-run protocol. The final experiment adds calibrated hysteresis and a sustained-return criterion. Natural dynamical deaths were too scarce to make this the same estimand as the MLP campaign.
