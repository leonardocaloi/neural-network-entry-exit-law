# Review 11: transformer protocol

**Status:** Historical protocol correction.

The review requires an explicit account of the depth confound in the GELU comparison and disclosure that hysteresis calibration uses frozen-attention runs. These limitations remain relevant to interpreting the final experiment. A depth contrast alone cannot identify a general GELU barrier, and the historical transfer label is not a proof that the fixed-readout theorem applies to this architecture.
