# Transformer confirmatory results

**Status:** Archived deterministic campaign: 800 runs.

The frozen-attention, shallow-injection softplus-beta-3 cell has 22 sustained returns out of 50 injected units, with a 95% Clopper-Pearson interval of [0.300, 0.587]. Area opening is recorded in 21 of those 22 returns. Hard ReLU has 20 sustained returns out of 100 with attention trainable and zero out of 100 with attention frozen. These results distinguish possible return channels within the tested architecture; they do not establish production-scale behavior.
