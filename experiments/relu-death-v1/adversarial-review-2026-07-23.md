# First ReLU toy: adversarial review

**Status:** Rejected historical design.

This review distinguishes identities that hold by construction from genuinely uncertain predictions. The first proposal was rejected as an experiment because its proposed discrimination did not establish the intended mechanism. Its lemmas and the failed design remain useful provenance for the subsequent stochastic toy studies and the deterministic entry-exit manuscript.
