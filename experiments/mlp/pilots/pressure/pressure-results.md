# MLP pressure pilot

**Status:** Exploratory results.

The pilot aggregates events by hidden width and activation, distinguishing units that start inactive from units that become inactive during training. Its purpose was to establish whether dynamical returns could be observed under capacity pressure and to inform a later study. Rare events in this pilot do not provide a confirmatory population-rate estimate.
