# MLP rate-pilot results

**Status:** Exploratory sizing evidence.

The rate pilot contains 240 runs across four activation arms, two widths, and 30 seeds. It distinguishes dynamical and initially inactive units, summarizes return times, and diagnoses mismatches between an earlier instrument and the ground-truth event counter. Its primary softplus arm supplied the eligibility window and sizing inputs for the later 400-run campaign. Pilot records must not be pooled into confirmatory evidence.
