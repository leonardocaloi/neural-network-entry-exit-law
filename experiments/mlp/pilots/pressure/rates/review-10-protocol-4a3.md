# Review 10: final MLP protocol

**Status:** Historical protocol correction.

The review identified that part of the event ordering is imposed by the ground-truth event clock. The revised C2 endpoint therefore asks whether area opening occurs during an inactive episode before a return, rather than presenting the imposed ordering as an empirical discovery. The final protocol retains all dynamical returns in the denominator and separates exploratory C3 analyses.
