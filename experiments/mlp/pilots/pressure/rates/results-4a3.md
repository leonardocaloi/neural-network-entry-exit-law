# MLP confirmatory results

**Status:** Archived deterministic campaign: 400 runs.

In the primary softplus-beta-3 arm, 16 of 35 dynamical deaths return; 15 of 30 eligible deaths return. Area opening is recorded in 13 of the 16 returns. The hard-ReLU validity arm has no returns among 17 dynamical deaths. The analysis reports the activation-sharpness comparison separately. These are small-network observations beyond the fixed-readout theorem assumptions; the manuscript gives the interpretation and uncertainty.
