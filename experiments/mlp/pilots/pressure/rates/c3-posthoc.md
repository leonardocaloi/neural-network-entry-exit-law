# C3: exploratory release-gap analysis

**Status:** Post-hoc analysis; not a new confirmatory endpoint.

This analysis examines the gap between recorded area opening and return, its relationship with inactive depth and activation sensitivity, and how much timing structure the instrument imposes. The report explicitly treats the analysis as post hoc. The frozen 4a.3 results and the current manuscript should be consulted before interpreting an apparent temporal relationship as an independent prediction.
