# Responses to MLP design reviews

**Status:** Historical author response record.

This document records responses to reviews 6 and 7, including changes to the instrument, calibration, pairing, and decision criteria. It is useful for reconstructing how the protocol evolved before the initial campaign. The later null-control failure remains part of the record and is not superseded by these responses.
