# Review 9: stage 4a.3 sizing

**Status:** Design correction requested.

The review found that the proposed return probability and sample size were not supported by the pilot. It required a rate pilot, an estimand conditional on observed dynamical deaths, explicit treatment of censoring, and a revised area endpoint. It also separated instrument validity from evidence about the proposed mechanism. The final 4a.3 protocol records the resulting decisions.
