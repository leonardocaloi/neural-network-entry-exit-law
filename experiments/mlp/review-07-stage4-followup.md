# Review 7: MLP design follow-up

**Status:** Historical review with a new validity blocker.

The follow-up reassessed the previous corrections and identified a structural bias in a delay-permuted placebo. A calibrated instrument must retain its claimed false-positive behavior under the relevant data-generating process. The review illustrates why resolving implementation defects did not, by itself, establish the validity of the experimental decision rule.
