# Stage 4: population-study design draft

**Status:** Historical design proposal.

The first MLP population-study draft translates toy-model mechanisms into questions about event rates, inactive-episode durations, and competing explanations. It explicitly discusses what can already be decided analytically, instrument design, population confounders, and minimal experimental scope. The initial 600-run campaign was subsequently invalidated by its own null control; its results do not establish the proposed mechanism.
