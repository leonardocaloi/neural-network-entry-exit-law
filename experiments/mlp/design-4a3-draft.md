# Stage 4a.3: early confirmatory design

**Status:** Superseded design.

This draft formulated existence, residual-area, and activation-sharpness questions for deterministic MLP training under increased capacity pressure. Review 9 found that its sizing assumptions were inconsistent with the pilot event rates. A separate rate pilot and revised eligibility-conditioned estimand followed. Use the final protocol in pilots/pressure/rates for the released campaign.
