# Review 18: trainable-readout draft

**Status:** Exploratory extension with recorded corrections.

The review identifies issues involving the inverse-readout power in a bound, the scope of the readout guard, and notation for a curvature bound. These findings concern an extension draft, not an additional theorem certified by the public release. The roadmap keeps trainable readouts as a distinct work item with proof obligations to resolve.
