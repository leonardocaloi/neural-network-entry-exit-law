# Review 15: forcing theorem draft

**Status:** Historical mathematical review.

This review examines the pre-entry error order, extraction of a sharpness threshold, the scope of the theorem, and agreement between the statement and the continuous-induction argument. It also discusses avoiding circular estimates. The publication review later amended a bootstrap step, so the current manuscript and release review take precedence over older conclusions.
