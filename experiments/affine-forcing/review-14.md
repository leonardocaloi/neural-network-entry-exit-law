# Review 14: affine forcing extension

**Status:** Historical mathematical review.

The review organizes the proof obligations for one inactive unit coupled to an affine live subsystem. It examines the dependence of estimates on width and forcing constants and records targeted numerical checks. The current statement and proof are in the manuscript; the original report preserves the numbered issues addressed during drafting.
