# ReLU toy v4: adversarial review

**Status:** Historical preregistration review.

The fifth local review examines the follow-up study of temperature-dependent residual effects and whether they reflect two-dimensional geometry or a modeling artifact. The frozen predictor and saved outputs are retained. This study is part of the historical stochastic toy program, not a validation of the later deterministic MLP and transformer campaigns.
