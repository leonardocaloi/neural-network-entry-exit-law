# ReLU toy v2: adversarial review

**Status:** Historical preregistration review.

Two rounds and a focused follow-up examined the reduced race model and the conditions for freezing its experimental design. The original record lists corrections and the remaining design issue. The subsequently executed model was falsified; passing a design review should not be confused with confirming its scientific hypothesis.
