# Lemma 4: Sticking and release bounds

**Status:** Historical proof-development note.

Uses prescribed residual forcing to study inactive depth and release bounds. Initialization directly at the boundary must be distinguished from the positive-margin initial state of the coupled entry-exit theorem.
